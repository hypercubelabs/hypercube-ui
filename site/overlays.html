Overlays

What opens on top of the page. They all close with Esc and with a tap outside, and give focus back to where it was.

Modal

.scrim .modal-wrap .modal .modal-h .modal-b .modal-f

For a decision or a short form. On phones it rises from the bottom.

Delete the project?

The 12 tasks and the project files go away for everyone. This cannot be undone.

Code
<button class="btn btn-secondary" data-open="#d-modal"><svg class="i" width="14" height="14"><!-- Lucide square-dashed, stroke 1.8 --></svg>Open the modal</button>
<div data-layer id="d-modal" hidden>
  <div class="scrim"></div>
  <div class="modal-wrap">
    <div class="modal sm" role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-labelledby="d-modal-t">
      <div class="modal-h"><h2 id="d-modal-t">Delete the project?</h2><button class="ibtn" data-close aria-label="Close"><svg class="i" width="16" height="16"><!-- Lucide x, stroke 1.8 --></svg></button></div>
      <div class="modal-b"><p style="margin:0;color:var(--text-2)">The 12 tasks and the project files go away for everyone. This cannot be undone.</p></div>
      <div class="modal-f"><span class="sp"></span><button class="btn btn-secondary" data-close>Cancel</button><button class="btn btn-danger" data-close data-toast="Nothing was deleted: this is only an example">Delete project</button></div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

Toast

.toasts[role=status] > .toast .ta .i.ok .i.bad .out

Confirms something that just happened. Lives in a fixed .toasts in the corner (on phones, at the bottom, full width); goes away on its own after 3 s with .out. If it can be undone, bring the .ta button.

Task archived
Code
<div class="row"><button class="btn btn-secondary" data-toast="Task archived"><svg class="i" width="14" height="14"><!-- Lucide bell, stroke 1.8 --></svg>Show a toast</button></div>
<div class="toasts" role="status" style="position:static;align-items:flex-start">
  <div class="toast"><svg class="i" width="15" height="15"><!-- Lucide circle-check, stroke 1.8 --></svg><span>Task archived</span><button class="ta" data-toast="Example: would undo">Undo</button></div>
</div>
