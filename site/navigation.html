Context switcher

.hc-switch .hc-switch-logo .hc-switch-name .pop.hc-switch-menu .mi[role=menuitemradio]

The workspace, organisation or product the person works in, the same way in every app. One per app, the first row of the menu (.side-top), in place of .hc-brand: the logo and name of the current one and the up-down chevrons. It opens a menu with every choice (logo, name, plan or role on the right), the current one checked, then what acts on them: settings, create, invite, only the ones that work. Choosing reloads the whole app in the new context. Never a select field, never in the top bar or inside a page.
